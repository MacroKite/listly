A calm home for the things people make.

Templates, ebooks, presets, fonts, courses, plugins. Creators paste a link and get a product page. Buyers find things here and buy on the creator’s own site.

01 · LINK FIRST
Most creators already sell somewhere
Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, their own site. Listing starts by pasting that link, and we fill in the title, cover and price from it.
02 · TRUST BEFORE THE CLICK
Show exactly where the link goes
Buyers can’t hold a file in their hands. Each page shows the destination domain, the file format, what works with it, and when it was last updated.
03 · A PAGE WORTH SHARING
Every product gets a clean URL
listly.co/p/… for each product and listly.co/@name for the creator. Easy to drop into a bio, a WhatsApp group or a Facebook post.
04 · CLICKS, NOT INQUIRIES
Stats creators actually need
Page views and link clicks per product. We check every link daily and say so in plain language when one breaks.
